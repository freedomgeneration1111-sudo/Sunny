# Focus Lab Productions — Master Offer & Pricing Decision Source

**Dallas–Fort Worth | Prepared August 13, 2026**  
**Market evidence consolidated through August 10, 2026**

## Purpose and authority

This is the single working offer-and-pricing decision file for Focus Lab Productions. It consolidates the project’s DFW competitor research, offer-architecture work, current website inventory, and pricing-development work into one review surface.

It has two jobs:

1. **Sunny reviews this file and decides what Focus Lab will actually sell, what each offer includes, and what public price or pricing mode applies.**
2. **After approval, the website/CRM pricing inventory should be generated from these decisions rather than from scattered development assumptions.**

The dollar figures in the market columns are **competitor evidence or normalized market bands, not Focus Lab prices**. Blank Focus Lab decision cells are intentionally blank until Sunny approves them.

### Evidence rule used in this file

Priority is: live primary-source competitor pricing/package pages and attributable social offers → corroborated current sources → transparent calculations → secondary directories → explicitly labeled low-confidence context. Simulated competitor names/prices from early exploratory work are excluded. Stale prices are replaced by later reconciled values where the project already resolved a conflict.

### Sunny review codes

For **SELL?**, mark one of:

- **YES** — publish and sell normally.
- **CUSTOM** — sell, but price only after event details are known.
- **LATER** — capability may exist but do not launch it yet.
- **NO** — do not offer.

For **PUBLIC PRICE MODE**, choose one of:

- **EXACT** — one clear public price under stated conditions.
- **STARTING** — “Starting at $X.”
- **HOURLY** — “$X/hour” or block pricing.
- **RANGE** — public expected range where useful.
- **CUSTOM** — inquiry/proposal required; a starting investment may still be shown.
- **INCLUDED** — bundled into another base offer, not separately priced.

# 5. What should normally remain custom quote

Unless Sunny deliberately standardizes these, the market research supports keeping them custom:

- multi-day or multi-venue South Asian celebrations;
- large LED walls, stage design and advanced production;
- large or technically unusual venues;
- custom dance floors and large production builds;
- complex live performer/live-sound requirements;
- multi-room corporate AV;
- destination/travel-heavy events;
- unusual crew scaling or specialized insurance/COI requirements;
- restrictive load-in, power, rigging or effects conditions;
- highly expedited or unusual same-day media deliverables;
- CO2/laser/large-effect packages where equipment, operator and venue conditions materially change cost.

“Custom” should not automatically mean “secret.” Where Sunny can define a credible floor, the site can still say **Starting at $X** and explain what changes the price.

# 8. Research reconciliation — claims deliberately NOT carried forward

The project generated several early exploratory claims that were later rejected or downgraded. They must not be allowed back into the website or pricing source by accident.

- **Do not use simulated competitor companies or simulated prices** from the early South Asian pricing sprint as evidence.
- **Do not assume a universal 30–60% South Asian premium.** Complexity and spend can be higher, but there is no defensible single percentage rule.
- **Do not make dhol, baraat support or bilingual MC mandatory in every South Asian package.** Offer them where applicable.
- **Do not claim exactly three packages is an industry standard.** Two, three, four and custom structures all exist.
- **Do not assume premium vendors always hide pricing.** Premium South Asian media firms do publish useful anchors.
- **Do not reuse stale Any Event pricing** of $1,150/$1,800/$2,850/$5,500. The reconciled project values are $1,500/$2,000/$3,500/$5,750.
- **Treat Monica Salazar headline photography-only starting values cautiously** because project research found inconsistent live-page values; detailed photo+video tiers are more reliable.
- **Do not treat DTX corporate AV pricing as the DFW market average.** It is useful detailed vendor evidence, but the market sample is too small.
